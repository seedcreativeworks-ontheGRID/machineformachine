Welcome aboard Galley OS. Here's how this works.

Set your total time frame at the top — how long until serve. Add each dish
you're cooking below: name, total cook time, ingredients, and your method.

Hit ENGAGE, and the system reverse-schedules everything — it works
backward from your serve time, so every dish finishes together, no matter
when it started.

Watch the LIVE TASKS panel up top. It shows exactly what to do right now,
for every dish, with a live countdown. When a task turns red, it's
urgent — act on it now.

Each dish card below has its own countdown and ingredients list — tap to
check off steps as you go.

Stuck mid-cook? Ask Galley AI. Type or tap the mic and ask anything —
substitutions, fixes, timing — it'll answer fast.

Set your time. Add your dishes. Hit engage. Cook to the clock.

Let's serve on time.
